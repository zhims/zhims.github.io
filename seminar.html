Yao Zhang - Seminar


This seminar series provides a forum for the presentation and discussion of recent advances in mathematics, physics, engineering, and computational science. Topics include mathematical modeling, numerical methods, scientific computing, data analysis, machine learning, and their applications to challenging problems in science and engineering. Through talks, research presentations, and open discussions, the seminar seeks to foster the exchange of ideas, encourage interdisciplinary collaboration, and provide researchers with opportunities to explore emerging methods, current developments, and open research questions.
